Field study · sand flat beside the reef, 4 m

Long-spine porcupinefish

Diodon holocanthus

It cruises on its dorsal and anal fins, hovers on its pectorals and steers with its tail. When threatened it gulps water into an elastic stomach, and the spines that lay flat against its skin stand straight out.
